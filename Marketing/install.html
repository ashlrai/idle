Installing Idle

Takes 2-3 minutes. macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, Apple Silicon recommended.

⬇︎ Download Idle.dmg (434 KB)
1

Open the DMG and drag Idle into Applications

Double-click Idle.dmg to mount it. Drag Idle.app onto the Applications shortcut. Eject the DMG.

About the "cannot be opened" warning

Idle is currently distributed without an Apple Developer ID notarization (it costs $99/year — we'll add it once we have meaningful users). When you first try to launch, macOS will show a warning. Idle is open source — you can read every line at github.com/ashlrai/idle before running.

To bypass the warning, follow steps 2-3 below.

2

Right-click Idle.app → Open

In Finder, navigate to /Applications. Right-click (or Control-click) Idle.app and choose Open from the menu. macOS will show the warning with an "Open" button this time.

Click Open. macOS remembers this choice; future launches happen normally.

3

Or use Terminal (faster)

If the right-click trick doesn't work (sometimes happens on macOS 14+), open Terminal and run:

xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Idle.app

This removes the "downloaded from internet" flag. Then double-click Idle.app to launch normally.

4

Look for the leaf in your menu bar

Idle is a menu-bar app (no Dock icon). After launch, you'll see Idle in the top-right of your screen. Click it to open the popover. The welcome wizard auto-opens on first launch with pre-flight checks and earnings expectations.

5

Toggle "Launch at login"

In the popover footer, flip the Launch at login switch. macOS will pop a permission dialog the first time — approve it. Idle now starts every time you log in.

If you have Homebrew

Power users can skip the Gatekeeper dance entirely:

brew tap ashlrai/idle https://github.com/ashlrai/idle
brew install --cask idle
open /Applications/Idle.app

Homebrew bypasses Gatekeeper because brew itself is trusted by macOS.

Uninstalling

defaults delete ai.ashlr.idle
rm -rf /Applications/Idle.app
launchctl unload ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.user.keepawake.plist 2>/dev/null
rm -f ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.user.keepawake.plist
