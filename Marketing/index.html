What we won't promise

If you've seen "$500/month passive income from your laptop," that's almost always survey stacking. Here's the honest version.

Hard limits — these need you, not the app

  • SMS verification (Pawns) — code is on your phone. Idle detects the OTP when you copy it; you click insert.
  • Email OTP / verification links — Idle detects them on the clipboard. v0.5 will read Gmail directly with your consent.
  • Phantom seed phrase for GRASS payouts — must be on paper.
  • Google OAuth signups — you click. Idle never holds your Google credentials.
  • Chrome Web Store extension installs — Chrome blocks scripted installs. We open the page; you click "Add to Chrome."

Risks you accept

  • Your residential ISP's terms may forbid bandwidth resale (Comcast/Spectrum).
  • Your IP becomes the egress for buyers' traffic (mostly AI training and BI scraping).
  • 24/7 plug-in degrades battery without the 80% charging limit.
  • Token income is taxable on receipt at fair-market value. Cash income is 1099-reportable above $600/yr.
